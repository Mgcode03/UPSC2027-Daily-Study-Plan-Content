## Absolute Must-Remember Facts

### Composition
- Original strength: 389 members
- After partition: 299 members
- Elected by Provincial Assemblies (indirect election)
- First meeting: 9 December 1946
- Temporary Chairman: Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha
- Permanent President: Dr. Rajendra Prasad
- Vice-President: H.C. Mukherjee
- Constitutional Advisor: B.N. Rau

### Objective Resolution
- Moved by: Jawaharlal Nehru
- Date moved: 13 December 1946
- Date adopted: 22 January 1947
- Later became the basis of the Preamble

### Drafting Committee
- Appointed: 29 August 1947
- Chairman: Dr. B.R. Ambedkar
- Members: 7 (including Ambedkar)
- First draft published: February 1948

### Final Timeline
- Constitution adopted: 26 November 1949
- Constitution came into force: 26 January 1950
- Time taken: 2 years 11 months 18 days
- Total sittings: 165 days
- Total sessions: 11

### Why 26 January?
Because the Congress had declared 26 January 1930 as Independence Day (Purna Swaraj resolution of Lahore Session, December 1929).

## One-Paragraph Summary (Write this from memory tomorrow)

The Constituent Assembly of India was formed under the Cabinet Mission Plan of 1946. It first met on 9 December 1946. Dr. Rajendra Prasad was its President and Dr. B.R. Ambedkar chaired the Drafting Committee. The Assembly worked for nearly three years, held 11 sessions and 165 sittings. The Constitution was adopted on 26 November 1949 and came into force on 26 January 1950. The Objective Resolution moved by Nehru in December 1946 formed the soul of the Preamble.
