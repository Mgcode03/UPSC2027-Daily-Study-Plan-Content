## Exam Overview (UPSC CSE 2027)

### Key Dates (as per UPSC Calendar)
- Notification: 13 January 2027
- Application: 13 Jan – 2 Feb 2027
- Prelims: 23 May 2027
- Mains: Starting 20 August 2027 (5 days)
- Interview: Early 2028

### Stages
1. **Prelims** (Screening)
   - GS Paper I: 100 questions, 200 marks, 2 hours (counts for cutoff)
   - CSAT Paper II: 80 questions, 200 marks, 2 hours (qualifying – 33% = 66.67 marks)
   - Negative marking: 1/3rd

2. **Mains** (Written – Merit)
   - 9 papers total
   - Qualifying: Indian Language (300), English (300)
   - Merit (1750 marks): Essay (250) + GS I–IV (250 each) + Optional I & II (250 each)

3. **Personality Test**: 275 marks

**Grand Total for Ranking**: 2025 marks

### Recent Prelims Cut-offs (General Category, out of 200)
- 2025: 92.66
- 2024: 87.98
- 2023: 75.41
- 2022: 88.22
- 2021: 87.54
- 2020: 92.51

Target for safety: Aim 105+ in mocks.

### High-Weight Subjects in Prelims GS (based on long-term trends)
Polity, Economy, History (esp. Modern + Art & Culture), Environment, Geography, Science & Tech, Current Affairs.
